## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides an end-to-end data analytics study evaluating over **290,000 Electric Vehicle (EV) registrations** sourced from the **Washington State Department of Licensing (DOL)** 

The primary objective is to track market adoption trajectories, examine brand concentration, evaluate zero-emission range capabilities across powertrains, and analyze infrastructure equity across legislative districts and electric utility service regions

----

## 🎯 Project Objectives
* **EV Adoption Curves:** Evaluate temporal growth trends and velocity shifts from 2010 to recent model years
* **Market Share Dominance:** Identify market share breakdowns between pure **Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs)** and **Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)** alongside top manufacturer volumes
* **Technological Progression:** Measure electric range distributions and performance advancements across vehicle generations[cite: 1].
* **Policy & Infrastructure Equity:** Analyze **Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV)** eligibility and regional adoption disparities between metropolitan corridors and rural districts

---

## 📊 Analytical Roadmap & Highlights

### 1. Univariate Analysis
* Distribution of top 10 EV manufacturers (Tesla, Chevrolet, Nissan, Ford, BMW.
* Market split between BEVs and PHEVs using interactive Plotly donut and polar charts.

### 2. Bivariate Analysis
* Temporal growth trajectories tracking registration surges post-2015.
* Box plot comparisons of electric range capabilities across BEVs and PHEVs.
* Manufacturer portfolio breakdown analyzing pure BEV strategy vs. hybrid diversification
### 3. Multivariate Analysis
* Cross-tabulation of CAFV eligibility status across top manufacturers using interactive 100% stacked bar plots and treemaps
* Geographic clustering maps identifying adoption concentration in King County and Puget Sound urban districts

---

## 💡 Key Strategic Findings
* **Powertrain Dominance:** BEVs hold a decisive lead over PHEVs as consumer preference aligns with expanding battery ranges and maturing charging networks
* **Manufacturer Concentration:** Market share remains heavily concentrated among top manufacturers, led by Tesla by a significant margin[.
* **Incentive Alignment:** The vast majority of registered vehicles meet CAFV eligibility criteria, while non-eligible vehicles are predominantly legacy short-range PHEVs
* **Geographic Disparities:** High adoption is strongly correlated with high-density urban technology hubs, highlighting a need for targeted fast-charging infrastructure in rural utility territories
